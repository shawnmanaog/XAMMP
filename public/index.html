Frequently asked questions

Select a question to expand.

How long does a passport take?

Regular takes about 20 working days from biometrics; Express about 7. Track live progress with your Reference No.

Can I change my appointment?

Yes, up to 24 hours before. Send an enquiry quoting your Reference No. with your preferred new date.

My application was rejected. What now?

Open the application to see the reason. Correct the issue and submit a new application — your fee is credited if rejected before biometrics.

How do I collect my passport?

Collect in person with your claim stub and ID, or choose courier delivery ($12). You will be notified when it reaches “Ready for Release”.
